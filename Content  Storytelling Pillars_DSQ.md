## **Deuxsentique – Content & Storytelling Pillars** 

## **Internal Brand & Creative Direction Document** 

_For marketing team, photographers, videographers, designers, and long-term brand consistency._ 

## **✨ Overview** 

The content strategy of Deuxsentique is built to create a **calm, cinematic, emotionally intelligent luxury world** . The goal is not to sell products directly but to build deep emotional connection, trust, and community. 

Deuxsentique content is designed to feel like a slow, unfolding film rather than advertising. Every piece of communication should reflect stillness, depth, mystery, and transformation. 

The brand speaks softly but with meaning. It whispers, not shouts. 

This document defines the emotional universe and creative pillars that guide all storytelling. 

## **✨ 1. Founder Presence & Mystery** 

The founder is the emotional core of Deuxsentique but will remain mostly behind the brand to maintain mystery and premium perception. 

The strategy is: 

- Mostly anonymous luxury founder 

- Rare and intentional visibility 

- No overexposure or daily personal content 

- Curiosity and intrigue around the founder identity 

The founder will: 

- Appear occasionally through hands, silhouette, or partial presence 

- Show the process, work, and struggles without revealing full personal life 

- Use voiceovers rather than frequent face-to-camera communication 

- Reveal the face and voice only at significant milestones 

For example: 

- A rare video after the soft batch launch to thank customers 

- Important updates or brand moments 

- Major launches or turning points 

- Approximately 1–2 visible appearances per year 

This creates: 

- Mystery 

- Authority 

- Luxury perception 

- Emotional curiosity 

This approach aligns with selective visibility strategies seen in premium founder-led brands. 

## **✨ 2. Founder Journey & Business Building** 

The brand will share selected aspects of the founder’s journey: 

- Starting from nothing and building a global luxury brand 

- Discipline, persistence, and long-term vision 

- Emotional struggles and resilience 

- Building systems and structure 

- The reality of entrepreneurship 

- Late nights, planning, and progress 

- Workspaces and brand environment 

- Behind-the-scenes creation 

The intention is not to overshare, but to inspire and guide. 

Personal life will remain private. The love story will be shared mainly during the _First Embrace_ chapter. 

This creates: 

- Trust 

- Relatability 

- Authority 

- Emotional connection 

## **✨ 3. Emotional Education** 

Deuxsentique will position itself as a brand that teaches emotional awareness and intentional living. 

Core themes include: 

- Emotional intelligence and self-awareness 

- How scent affects memory and mood 

- The psychology of fragrance 

- Ritual creation and mindful living 

- Identity and personal expression 

- Slowing down in a fast world 

- Confidence through subtle luxury 

- Emotional strength and calm 

- Personal growth and transformation 

- The connection between scent, memory, and behaviour 

The goal is to become a **thought leader in emotional fragrance** . 

## **✨ 4. Cinematic Storytelling & Visual Mood** 

The visual direction of Deuxsentique is cinematic and poetic. 

Key moods: 

- Dark and mysterious 

- Minimal and modern 

- Nature and silence 

- Soft romantic and emotional 

- Slow and dreamlike 

- Calm and atmospheric 

Visual elements include: 

- Natural light 

- Textures and shadows 

- Soft focus and depth 

- Quiet, intimate environments 

- Movement and stillness 

- Emotional storytelling rather than product focus 

Every piece of content should feel like a scene from a film. 

## **✨ 5. Ritual & Slow Luxury** 

Deuxsentique will educate customers to treat fragrance as a ritual, not a routine. 

Content themes: 

- Morning and evening scent rituals 

- Fragrance as emotional grounding 

- Mindful application 

- Linking scent to intention 

- Creating personal moments of stillness 

- Connecting perfume to daily life transitions 

This reinforces: 

- Slow luxury 

- Emotional depth 

- Brand loyalty 

## 🌍 **6. Community & Emotional Connection** 

Deuxsentique is not a transactional brand. It is a community. 

The goal is to make customers feel: 

- Safe 

- Calm 

- Guided 

- Inspired 

- Intellectually stimulated 

- Emotionally supported 

- Exclusive yet welcoming 

The brand will: 

- Encourage sharing of personal stories 

- Create emotional conversations 

- Highlight customer experiences 

- Build belonging and identity 

- Foster long-term loyalty 

## **✨ 7. Behind-the-Scenes & Craft** 

Craft and transparency will be shown selectively. 

Content will include: 

- Fragrance development 

- Packaging process 

- Quality and detail 

- Lab and formulation insights 

- Sampling and testing 

- Supplier and production journey 

The intention is to show authenticity without reducing luxury perception. 

## **✨ 8. Customer Transformation** 

Deuxsentique content will focus on transformation rather than product features. 

The brand helps customers move: 

- From emotional confusion → clarity 

- From insecurity → calm confidence 

- From noise → stillness 

- From pain → resilience 

- From superficial identity → authentic self 

- From distraction → presence 

This makes fragrance a tool for growth. 

## **✨ 9. Content Outcome & Strategic Role** 

This pillar system ensures: 

- Consistent luxury communication 

- Emotional depth in every post 

- Strong brand recognition 

- High-value community growth 

- Organic trust and loyalty 

- Premium perception 

- Long-term cultural positioning 

**Deuxsentique content is not about selling perfume. It is about building an emotional world where the right souls belong.**
