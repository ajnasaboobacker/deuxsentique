## 2. Origin Story

Deuxsentique was founded in the United Kingdom by two kindred spirits:

- **Resten** — a storyteller with a gift for turning life into narrative; a dreamer with structured vision; a builder of legacies. He sees the invisible threads connecting past and present, and weaves them into brand, philosophy, and legacy.
- **Jiji** — a chemist with deep knowledge of molecules and memory, able to turn science into soul. She understands the invisible bonds between scent and emotion, bringing technical mastery into harmony with feeling.

Their love story is the heartbeat of the brand. It began with handwritten notes, the scent of books and fabrics, and quiet mornings together. They started gifting perfumes to each other — testing fragrances on skin, waiting for the drydown, breathing in the lingering trace on each other's necks. These small, intimate rituals became the brand's first lesson: **perfume is not just worn — it is experienced, remembered, and shared.**

From this, Deuxsentique was born: two creators, one shared essence.

**Where chemistry meets soul:** Jiji ensures precision, safety, and authenticity; Resten shapes each perfume as a living story — a bottle of memory. Together they create fragrances that are technically balanced *and* emotionally profound.

**What perfume means here:** not a product, but emotion made wearable — a memory made physical, a feeling of love, intimacy, and connection preserved in liquid form.

**Craft principle:** principle over scale, craft over convenience. Hand-poured, hand-assembled, finished with care, produced in carefully controlled small batches. Even when collaborating with international artisans, every bottle is inspected, perfected, and treated as an individual piece of art.

**Cultural rooting:** founded in the UK, drawing poetic influence from French perfumery and culture. French elegance (timeless, romantic, poetic) balanced with British minimalism (refined, clean, honest) — modern and timeless, emotional and precise at once.

**What Deuxsentique stands for:** emotion over fashion; craftsmanship over mass production; connection over slogans; quiet luxury over noise.

## 3. Vision, Mission, Core Values

**Vision:** To become a timeless house of storytelling perfumes — where every fragrance is a keeper of memory, crafted with integrity, and destined to be cherished. To redefine luxury not as excess, but as emotional depth: perfumes that whisper, connect, and endure.

**Mission:** Deuxsentique exists to turn real stories into fragrances. It creates unisex extrait de parfums that begin with life — moments of love, resilience, discovery, and memory — and transforms them into scents that every soul can wear. The mission is to bottle emotions with precision and artistry, in small-batch perfumes that are soulful, cruelty-free, responsibly sourced, and unforgettable.

**Core values / brand pillars:**

1. **Emotion First** — every perfume begins with a lived story or memory; authenticity is the foundation.
2. **Quietly Luxurious** — small-batch, hand-finished creations reflecting detail, integrity, and soulful refinement.
3. **Authentic Connection** — perfumes that whisper rather than shout, designed to touch the soul, not follow trends.
4. **Ethical Elegance** — cruelty-free, vegan-conscious, with responsible sourcing at the heart of the craft.
5. **Legacy of Storytelling** — building a house of memories, a legacy of emotion preserved in scent.
6. **Inclusive & Unisex** — every fragrance crafted for all souls, beyond gender, beyond borders.

## 6. Ideal Emotional Customer

Not defined by age, gender, or status — defined by **emotional depth, self-awareness, and intentional living.** Seeks meaning, connection, and quiet transformation rather than trends or external validation. Sees fragrance as an extension of identity and a tool for personal grounding. Community built on soul, memory, and evolution.

**Core personality:**
- Calm, thoughtful, emotionally intelligent
- Introverted or selectively social; prefers depth over noise
- Reflective, self-aware, curious about life's deeper layers
- Creatives, artists, visionaries, builders
- Spiritual yet modern and grounded
- Structured and disciplined
- Positive, future-focused, growth-oriented
- Feels different, and embraces uniqueness
- Avoids drama, superficiality, artificial environments

Naturally resonates with brands like **Byredo, Aesop, and Le Labo**, while seeking deeper emotional storytelling and founder connection than those brands typically offer.

**Emotional stage of life:** in transition and self-development — discovering identity, healing from past experience, building a career or creative path, searching for meaning, developing discipline and self-respect, choosing quality over quantity, focused on long-term growth over short-term pleasure.

**Emotional outcome they seek when wearing Deuxsentique:** grounded and present; calm and balanced; deep and connected to their inner world; quietly powerful and confident; unique without seeking attention; safe and emotionally secure; guided; grateful; connected to memory, identity, and purpose.

**Lifestyle & behaviour:** mentorship and learning; art, creativity, culture; journaling and reflection; physical discipline (gym, yoga, wellness); minimal and refined fashion; travel for perspective, not status; reading and curiosity; mental/emotional wellbeing; nature, silence, simplicity. They do not display wealth or status — they cultivate inner richness.

**Relationship with luxury:** true luxury is calm, not loud; craftsmanship matters more than logos; story and meaning create value; emotional connection builds loyalty; "less but better" is the future; identity matters more than trends. They are less price-sensitive when they feel understood and connected.

**Community & belonging:** the brand acts as a safe emotional space, a symbol of identity, a source of inspiration and calm, a reminder of personal transformation. Customers are framed as long-term believers and ambassadors, not buyers — they share the brand organically.

**Guiding line:** *"Deuxsentique does not seek everyone. It seeks the right souls."*

## 7. Tone of Voice & Style Guide

**Overall personality:**
- Poetic & Emotional — every word should touch the heart, like a story whispered on skin
- Elegant & Minimal — no clutter, no over-explaining; less is more
- Authentic & Human — speak as a soul, not a corporate machine
- Confident but Gentle — quiet luxury; resonate, don't shout

**Core principles:**

1. **Poetic storytelling** — use metaphor and imagery (light, shadow, embrace, memory, whisper); speak of emotion before product. *Example: "Not just worn, but remembered."*
2. **Quiet luxury** — avoid loud sales language; write with refinement. *Instead of "long-lasting power!" → "crafted to linger, softly and surely."*
3. **Intimacy & connection** — write as if speaking to one person, not a crowd. *Example: "This perfume is for the soul who…"*
4. **Universal emotion** — personal stories expressed so anyone can see themselves in them. Avoid overusing "I"/"we" → shift toward "you" and "the soul who…"

**Do:**
- Use emotional vocabulary: memory, essence, soul, intimacy, connection, resilience, light, shadow, rebirth, embrace
- Keep sentences short, elegant, flowing
- Lead with emotion, then notes

**Don't:**
- Use cliché marketing words: cheap, trendy, "for men/women," "the best," "must-have"
- Overcomplicate with technical jargon
- Sound corporate, pushy, or generic

**Tone examples by channel:**
- **Website:** "Every perfume begins as a story. Worn on the skin, it becomes yours."
- **Social caption:** "Our hearts already knew: this was more than a date, it was the beginning of forever. First Embrace — a story bottled."
- **Ad copy:** "A memory. A fragrance. A soul reborn."
- **Packaging/insert card:** "Thank you for carrying a piece of our essence. Two Souls. One Essence."

## 8. Content & Storytelling Pillars

Content is built to feel like a slow, unfolding film rather than advertising — stillness, depth, mystery, transformation. Goal is emotional connection and community, not direct selling.

1. **Founder presence & mystery** — mostly anonymous luxury founder; rare, intentional visibility (roughly 1–2 visible appearances per year); appears through hands, silhouette, or partial presence; voiceover over face-to-camera; full face/voice reveal reserved for major milestones (e.g., a rare video after the soft-batch launch). Creates mystery, authority, luxury perception, emotional curiosity.

2. **Founder journey & business building** — share discipline, persistence, long-term vision, emotional struggles and resilience, systems and structure, the reality of entrepreneurship, workspaces and behind-the-scenes creation. Not oversharing — intended to inspire and guide. Personal life stays private; the love story is shared mainly through the *First Embrace* chapter.

3. **Emotional education** — position the brand as a thought leader in emotional fragrance: emotional intelligence, how scent affects memory and mood, the psychology of fragrance, ritual creation, mindful living, identity and expression, slowing down, confidence through subtle luxury, personal growth.

4. **Cinematic storytelling & visual mood** — dark and mysterious; minimal and modern; nature and silence; soft romantic and emotional; slow and dreamlike; calm and atmospheric. Visual elements: natural light, texture and shadow, soft focus and depth, quiet intimate environments, movement and stillness. Every piece of content should feel like a scene from a film.

5. **Ritual & slow luxury** — treat fragrance as ritual, not routine: morning/evening scent rituals, fragrance as emotional grounding, mindful application, linking scent to intention, connecting perfume to daily transitions.

6. **Community & emotional connection** — not transactional; a community. Customers should feel safe, calm, guided, inspired, intellectually stimulated, emotionally supported, exclusive yet welcoming. Encourage sharing of personal stories and customer experiences.

7. **Behind-the-scenes & craft (selective)** — fragrance development, packaging process, quality and detail, lab/formulation insights, sampling and testing, supplier and production journey — shown to build authenticity without reducing luxury perception.

8. **Customer transformation** — content focuses on transformation, not product features: emotional confusion → clarity; insecurity → calm confidence; noise → stillness; pain → resilience; superficial identity → authentic self; distraction → presence.

9. **Strategic outcome** — this system is designed to produce consistent luxury communication, emotional depth in every post, strong brand recognition, high-value community growth, organic trust, premium perception, and long-term cultural positioning.

**Guiding line:** *"Deuxsentique content is not about selling perfume. It is about building an emotional world where the right souls belong."*

## 9. Product Line — The Founding Edition

The Founding Edition is Deuxsentique's first collection and the beginning of the brand's journey — created in limited quantities, marking the first chapter of a house built on real stories, emotions, and memories. Every SKU in this collection carries the same story and the same fragrance notes; they differ in format, concentration ritual, and occasion (solo wear, daily ritual, gifting, or full experience).

### The Story Behind First Embrace

First Embrace captures the emotion of two strangers becoming part of each other's lives — a moment filled with anticipation, warmth, comfort, and quiet connection. It was inspired by a real moment that marked the beginning of a meaningful connection: the anticipation before meeting, the warmth of closeness, the comfort of being understood, and the feeling that remains long after the moment has passed. Rather than recreating the memory literally, the fragrance captures the emotions surrounding it, following the house philosophy: **Story → Emotion → Notes → Perfume.**

The fragrance itself is designed to be intimate rather than overpowering — it unfolds gracefully on the skin, revealing layers of freshness, soft florals, and warm sensuality. Instead of filling a room, it invites discovery through closeness.

### Fragrance Notes (shared across the entire First Embrace line)

| Layer | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Top** | Bergamot, Mandarin, Jasmine |
| **Heart** | Damask Rose, Peony, Lychee |
| **Base** | Sandalwood, White Musk, Vanilla, Tonka Bean |

**How to wear (Extrait de Parfum format):** Spray onto pulse points — wrists, neck, behind the ears — from a short distance. Allow it to settle naturally without rubbing, letting the scent evolve throughout the day.

**How to wear (Roll-On format):** Gently apply to pulse points — wrists, neck, behind the ears. Allow the oil to settle naturally into the skin. Reapply throughout the day to reconnect with the story.

**Recurring closing line across the collection:**
*"A fragrance that belongs to you before it belongs to the room. Two Souls. One Essence."*

## 10. Visual & Design Direction

Distilled from the Content & Storytelling Pillars and applied in the current waitlist landing page design system.

**Visual mood:** dark and mysterious · minimal and modern · nature and silence · soft romantic and emotional · slow and dreamlike · calm and atmospheric. Every piece of content should feel like a scene from a film, not an advertisement.

**Visual elements to favor:** natural light; texture and shadow; soft focus and depth; quiet, intimate environments; a sense of movement within stillness; emotional storytelling over product-forward shots.

**Founder imagery rule:** hands, silhouette, or partial presence only — never full, frequent face-forward content. Voiceover over on-camera speech.

**Working design system (from the current landing page build):**

| Token | Value | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Void | `#120F12` | Primary background — near-black, warm plum undertone |
| Plum Smoke | `#211923` / `#19131A` | Section and card backgrounds |
| Ember | `#C6874F` | Primary accent — amber/liquid gold; used sparingly (CTAs, key words, top notes) |
| Rose Dust | `#A97C74` | Secondary accent — dusty rose; paired with Ember to echo "two souls" visually |
| Parchment | `#F0E8DA` | Primary text on dark backgrounds |
| Ash | `#8F8479` | Secondary/muted text, captions, dividers |

- **Display typeface:** Fraunces (serif, soft/poetic, used for wordmark, headlines, and emotional lines — italic for the tagline and quotes)
- **Body/utility typeface:** Work Sans (clean, minimal, used for body copy and uppercase-tracked eyebrow labels)
- **Signature motif:** two overlapping circles ("Two Souls. One Essence.") used as a recurring mark — in navigation, as section dividers, and as an ambient glowing motif in the hero
- **Motion principle:** slow, ambient, breathing — ombré light drifting, scroll-triggered fades, never abrupt or bouncy; `prefers-reduced-motion` respected throughout
