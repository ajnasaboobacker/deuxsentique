## **Deuxsentique – Ideal Emotional Customer** 

## **Internal Brand Document** 

_For in-house strategy, marketing, creative direction, and community building._ 

## **✨ Overview** 

The Deuxsentique customer is not defined by age, gender, or status. They are defined by **emotional depth, self-awareness, and intentional living** . 

Deuxsentique attracts individuals who seek meaning, connection, and quiet transformation rather than trends or external validation. They value inner growth, authenticity, and emotional intelligence. They see fragrance not as decoration, but as an extension of identity and a tool for personal grounding. 

Our community is built on **soul, memory, and evolution** . 

## **✨ 1. Core Personality & Inner World** 

The Deuxsentique individual is calm, thoughtful, and emotionally intelligent. They are often introverted or selectively social, preferring depth over noise. They are reflective, self-aware, and curious about life’s deeper layers. 

They are: 

- Introverted but not withdrawn or negative 

- Deep thinkers who reflect on life and meaning 

- Creatives, artists, visionaries, and builders 

- Spiritual yet modern and grounded 

- Calm, intelligent, and emotionally mature 

- Structured and disciplined in their daily life 

- Positive, future-focused, and growth-oriented 

- Friendly, respectful, and sincere 

- Individuals who feel different and embrace their uniqueness 

- People who avoid drama, superficiality, and artificial environments 

They are drawn to authenticity, purpose, and quiet confidence. They believe in continuous evolution and are attracted to brands that support their inner journey. 

This personality group naturally resonates with the emotional worlds of brands such as Byredo, Aesop, and Le Labo, while seeking deeper emotional storytelling and founder connection. 

## **✨ 2. Emotional Stage of Life** 

The Deuxsentique customer is often in a phase of **transition and self-development** . They are becoming more intentional in how they live, what they consume, and who they surround themselves with. 

They are: 

- Discovering their identity and personal truth 

- Healing from past experiences and emotional challenges 

- Building careers, businesses, or creative paths 

- Searching for deeper meaning and purpose 

- Developing discipline and self-respect 

- Attracted to nature, simplicity, and balance 

- Becoming more conscious about relationships and time 

- Choosing quality over quantity 

- Focused on long-term growth rather than short-term pleasure 

They value connection with loved ones and emotional stability. They are increasingly selective, thoughtful, and aligned with their values. 

This aligns strongly with Deuxsentique’s philosophy of **slow, intentional luxury** . 

## **✨ 3. Emotional Outcome & Transformation** 

Deuxsentique fragrances are not worn only to smell good. They serve as emotional anchors and daily rituals. 

When wearing Deuxsentique, the individual wants to feel: 

- Grounded and present 

- Calm and emotionally balanced 

- Deep and connected to their inner world 

- Quietly powerful and confident 

- Unique without seeking attention 

- Safe and emotionally secure 

- Guided in their journey 

- Grateful and appreciative of life 

- Connected to memory, identity, and purpose 

Each fragrance becomes a moment of stillness in a fast world. It is a reminder of who they are becoming. 

Deuxsentique represents: 

- Emotional clarity 

- Personal growth 

- Inner strength 

- Identity and authenticity 

## **✨ 4. Lifestyle & Behaviour** 

The Deuxsentique community lives a slow, intentional, and elevated lifestyle rooted in growth and self-awareness. 

They are drawn to: 

- Mentorship, learning, and personal development 

- Art, creativity, and culture 

- Journaling and reflection 

- Physical discipline such as gym, yoga, or wellness routines 

- Minimal and refined fashion 

- Travel for perspective and growth, not status 

- Reading, knowledge, and curiosity 

- Mental and emotional wellbeing 

- Nature, silence, and simplicity 

They do not seek to display wealth or status. Instead, they cultivate **inner richness and depth** . 

They value quality, craftsmanship, and meaningful experiences. 

## **✨ 5. Relationship with Luxury** 

For the Deuxsentique individual, luxury is not excess or noise. Luxury is emotional depth, time, and intention. 

They believe: 

- True luxury is calm, not loud 

- Craftsmanship matters more than logos 

- Story and meaning create value 

- Emotional connection builds loyalty 

- Less but better is the future 

- Identity is more important than trends 

They invest in products that: 

- Support their growth 

- Reflect their personality 

- Feel timeless and authentic 

- Align with their values 

They are less price-sensitive when they feel understood and connected. 

## **✨ 6. Community & Belonging** 

Deuxsentique is not just a brand. It is a community of individuals who feel seen and understood. 

The brand acts as: 

- A safe emotional space 

- A symbol of identity 

- A source of inspiration and calm 

- A reminder of personal transformation 

Customers are not buyers. 

They are long-term believers and ambassadors. 

They share the brand organically because they resonate deeply with its philosophy. 

## **✨ Strategic Importance** 

This emotional customer foundation will guide: 

- Marketing and communication 

- Social media tone and visuals 

- Influencer partnerships 

- Founder personal branding 

- Product storytelling 

- Community building 

- Website messaging 

- Campaign direction 

- Global expansion 

This audience is smaller but highly loyal, with strong lifetime value and word-of-mouth potential. 

**Deuxsentique does not seek everyone. It seeks the right souls.**
