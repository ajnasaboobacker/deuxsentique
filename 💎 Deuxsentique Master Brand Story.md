## **Deuxsentique – Brand Story** 

Tagline: Two Souls. One Essence. 

A fragrance house born from love, crafted with precision and story, and designed to touch the soul. 

## 🕯 **The Beginning – A Story of Love and Purpose** 

Deuxsentique was founded in the United Kingdom by two kindred spirits: 

- Resten – a storyteller with a gift for turning life into narrative, a dreamer with structured vision, and a builder of legacies. He sees the invisible threads that connect past and present, weaving them into brand, philosophy, and legacy. 

- Jiji – a chemist with deep knowledge of molecules and memory, able to turn science into soul. She understands the invisible bonds between scent and emotion, and brings technical mastery into harmony with feeling. 

Their love story is the heartbeat of the brand. What began with handwritten notes, the scent of books and fabrics, and quiet mornings together soon turned into something more. They began gifting perfumes to each other, testing fragrances on skin, waiting for the drydown, and breathing in the lingering traces on each other’s necks. 

These small, intimate rituals became the first lesson: perfume is not just worn — it is experienced, remembered, and shared. 

From this, Deuxsentique was born. A name uniting the French “Deux” (two) with the coined word “sentique”, representing scent as a language of memory, love, and presence. At its heart, the name carries their truth: Two creators. One shared essence. 

## ✨ **The Essence of Deuxsentique** 

Deuxsentique is more than a perfume house — it is a philosophy of scent as memory, emotion, and legacy. 

Every creation begins with a story: a lived moment, a memory, a chapter of emotion. Childhood corridors filled with scent, the spark of first connection, the strength to rise after loss, the quiet beauty of unspoken mornings. These fragments of life are not imagined — they are real. 

Each perfume transforms them into something universal, allowing every soul to carry a piece of emotion on their skin. 

## ✨ **Where Chemistry Meets Soul** 

At Deuxsentique, chemistry and creativity exist in perfect balance. Jiji ensures precision, safety, and authenticity, while Resten shapes each perfume as a living story — a bottle of memory. 

Together, they create fragrances that are not only technically balanced but emotionally profound — perfumes that whisper, not shout, leaving a lasting imprint of intimacy and depth. 

Perfume, for Deuxsentique, is not just a product — it is emotion made wearable. A memory made physical. A feeling of love, intimacy, and connection preserved in liquid form.

## ✨ **Crafted in Small Batches — With Integrity** 

Deuxsentique is proudly built on principle over scale, and craft over convenience. Every fragrance is produced in carefully controlled batches to preserve its soul and integrity. 

- Hand-poured, hand-assembled, and finished with care. 

- Cruelty-free, vegan-conscious, and ethically made. 

- Designed with detail — from bottle to cap, from atomiser to box — to reflect soulful luxury. 

Even when collaborating with international artisans, the human touch remains central. Each bottle is inspected, perfected, and treated as an individual piece of art.

This balance makes Deuxsentique a house that is both modern and timeless, emotional and precise.
