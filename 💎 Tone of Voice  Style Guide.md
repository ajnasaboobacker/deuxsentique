## **💎 Deuxsentique – Tone of Voice & Style Guide** 

## **💎 Overall Personality** 

- **Poetic & Emotional** – every word should touch the heart, like a story whispered on skin. 

- **Elegant & Minimal** – no clutter, no over-explaining; less is more. 

- **Authentic & Human** – speak as a soul, not as a corporate machine. 

- **Confident but Gentle** – quiet luxury; we don’t shout, we resonate. 

## **💎 Core Principles of Voice** 

## 1. **Poetic Storytelling** 

   - Use metaphors and imagery (light, shadow, embrace, memory, whisper). 

   - Speak of emotions before products. 

   - Example: _“Not just worn, but remembered.”_ 

2. **Quiet Luxury** 

   - Avoid loud sales language. 

   - Speak with refinement — elegant, understated, timeless. 

   - Example: instead of _“long-lasting power!”_ , say _“crafted to linger, softly and surely.”_ 

3. **Intimacy & Connection** 

   - Write as if speaking directly to one person, not to a crowd. 

   - Example: _“This perfume is for the soul who…”_ 

4. **Universal Emotion** 

   - Stories are personal but expressed in a way everyone can see themselves in them. 

   - Avoid too much “I” or “we” → shift to “you” and “the soul who…” 

## **💎 Do’s & Don’ts** 

## ✅ **Do** 

- Use emotional keywords: memory, essence, soul, intimacy, connection, resilience, light, shadow, rebirth, embrace. 

- Keep sentences short, elegant, and flowing. 

- Focus on emotions → then notes. 

## ✅ **Don’t** 

- Use cliché marketing words: cheap, trendy, “for men/women,” “the best,” “musthave.” 

- Overcomplicate with too much technical jargon. 

- 

- Sound corporate, pushy, or generic.
