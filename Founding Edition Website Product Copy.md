## **PRODUCT 01** 

## **Discovery Kit – First Embrace** 

## **(2 × 5 ml Discovery Set)** 

## **Hero Description** 

Discover the beginning of First Embrace. 

The Discovery Kit includes two 5 ml bottles, allowing you to experience the fragrance yourself while sharing the second with someone special. Inspired by the belief that meaningful stories are best experienced together, this set offers a beautiful introduction to the world of Deuxsentique. 

## **The Story** 

Every fragrance at Deuxsentique begins with a real story. 

First Embrace captures the emotion of two strangers becoming part of each other’s lives—a moment filled with anticipation, warmth, comfort and quiet connection. Rather than creating fragrance from trends, we transformed a genuine human experience into scent through our signature philosophy: 

**Story → Emotion → Notes → Perfume.** 

## **The Fragrance** 

First Embrace is an Extrait de Parfum created to remain close to the wearer. Smooth, elegant and deeply personal, it unfolds gently throughout the day, becoming part of your own story rather than announcing itself to a room.
